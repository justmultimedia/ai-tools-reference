In Ireland, you do have to give your spouse a part of your estate and you have to give them something under your will. If you have children, they're entitled to one third of your estate and this is called the legal right share. If you do not have children, they're entitled to half of your estate and this is their legal right share. If you do not leave anything to them in your will, this is an automatic entitlement that gets vested in them. If you leave them something small in your will that does not equal to the legal right share, then they have to make a decision about taking the gift you've offered in the will or taking the legal right share instead, which may be more. So talk to your spouse, talk to your solicitor and make sure that you know what you're doing when you're making your will.
