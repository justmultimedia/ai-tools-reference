Title: Don’t Miss Out On This When Going From Sole Trader to Limited Company 🇮🇪
Channel: Ask About Wealth
Transcript: published captions

Are you thinking of moving from a sole tradership to a limited company? Well, first of all, if you are, I'm behind you, because I believe limited companies are much more tax efficient in the longer term. But what I want you to watch out for is make sure that when you do move, you count your years of service in the sole tradership within the limited company. Known as TUPE, Transfer of Undertaking for Protection of Employment, what this will allow you to do later on is count the years of service in the sole tradership when calculating benefits from the limited company. Benefits like entrepreneurial relief, benefits like retirement relief, and benefits like retirement funds themselves. The devil is always in the detail. And the detail here is make sure, if you're trying to move, transferring from a sole tradership to a limited company, that your years of service in the sole tradership come with you.
