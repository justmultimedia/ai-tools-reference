Title: Don’t Underestimate What Happens If You Die Without a Will
Channel: Ask About Wealth
Transcript: published captions

Do you know what will happen to your wealth if you die without a will? Most people I meet on consoles every week don't seem to know these things. Here's what will happen. If you're married, you've no will, two-thirds of your assets will go to your spouse, one-third will go to your children. I met a couple recently where the gentleman had 2.5 million worth of assets. The couple weren't married. They had three children. If he died without a will, his kids didn't inherit everything. If you are a single person without kids and without a spouse and you die without a will, your mom and dad get everything. People don't seem to realize that a will is a vitally important part of your plan, your financial planning. It is the first cog in the succession planning wheel. Only 27% of Irish adults have a will. This is a call out to the 73% who don't. If you have nothing, you don't need to make a will. If you have any sort of assets at all, you do.
