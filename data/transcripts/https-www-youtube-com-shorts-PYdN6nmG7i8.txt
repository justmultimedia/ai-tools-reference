There are coding tools that don't suck. Here is one, Clerk. Authentication is usually painful. Clerk makes it simple. Users can sign in however they want, email, social login, pass keys, whatever fits your app. You get ready-made components so you're not building login screens from scratch. It also gives you user profiles, account management, and even billing if you want to charge people. Setup takes a few minutes, and if you're too lazy to wire everything manually, they even have an AI prompt that can do most of it for you. So if your app needs auth, don't waste a week building it badly.
