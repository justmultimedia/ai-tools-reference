Transcript: published captions

China just released a free open source AI video model that can generate unlimited length videos. Not just 15 second clips like every other tool, it's called Skyreels V2 built by Skywork AI and released fully open source on GitHub. The reason it can keep generating past the 15 second wall, most models hit is a technique called diffusion forcing, which uses what already happened in the video to decide what comes next, so the clip extends without needing to stitch separate generations together. You can feed it a text prompt or a reference image, and the visual quality lands close to paid tools like Kling and Runway. Two ways to use it, download the weights from Hugging Face and run it on your own GPU, or use the official Skyreels site with free credits included. Local run needs an RTX 4090 or better, so if you don't have that, the website is the easier path.
