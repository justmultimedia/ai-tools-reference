Title: With 40 Years In The Industry This Is The Biggest Tax Mistake I See 🇮🇪
Channel: Ask About Wealth
Transcript: published captions

If I was asked, what in my 40 plus years experience, what is the main culprit in terms of people overpaying taxation? The answer is the ignorance of the advisors that they've used. I can't tell you how many times I meet people whose accountant hasn't told them about SBE or subsistence allowances or loan goods. How many times I meet people who've written wills where the solicitor hasn't told them that the particular structure they're using is inappropriate for their familial circumstances. How many times I talk to people who've had a meeting with a bank manager to partner up when the bank manager's job is to flog you as much money as possible at as high a margin as possible. These are not your partners. The The strategy of abdicating responsibility would be fine if their agenda was your agenda. But it's not. Their agenda is something different. You need to be receiving financial guidance from somebody whose agenda is the same as yours. Somebody who truly understands what that agenda is and formulates their instructions and their suggestions based on that agenda. That is not possible from most accountants and most lawyers and most bank managers and indeed most so-called financial advisors. It's only possible from impartial financial planners.
