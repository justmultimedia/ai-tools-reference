Google has more free AI tools than Chat GPT, Anthropic, and Perplexity combined, and most people don't even know they exist. First is Nano Banana Pro, Google's image generation model that can create realistic images, mock-ups, posters, infographics, and 4K visuals. Next is Notebook LM, which turns your documents, links, and notes into summaries, AI-hosted podcasts, reports, and video overviews. Third is Opal, Google's no-code AI app builder. It lets you describe an automation or mini app in plain English, and it turns it into a visual workflow. Fourth is Pameli, which scans your website, learns your brand colors, tone, fonts, and style, then generates on-brand social posts and campaign ideas. Fifth is Gemini Canvas, where you can turn reports and rough ideas into apps, quizzes, infographics, web pages, and presentation-style outputs. Sixth is Stitch, Google's AI design tool that can generate full app and website interfaces from a single prompt. And seventh is Build in Google AI Studio, where you can describe a web app or Android app in plain English and start building it with Gemini.
