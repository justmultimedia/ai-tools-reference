Title: What skills will matter in 10 years? | Yuval Noah Harari
Channel: Yuval Noah Harari 
Transcript: published captions

Any attempt to focus on a narrow set of skills is dangerous. The best strategy that I can advise to people is to do the opposite, to have a broad set of skills. Generally speaking, we are talking about skills of the brain, of the heart, and of the hands. Intellectual skills, and emotional and social skills, and motor skills, physical skills. A combination of these three is the best strategy of how to cope with what is happening. Again, we don't know what kind of jobs people will have in 10 years, or what kind of skills they will need, but a combination of these three is the safest bet. You know, with intellectual skills, we don't know if people will need to code or to know mathematics, but the emotional and social skills will definitely be extremely important, because, when the world is changing faster and faster, the psychological pressures become bigger and bigger, and maybe the most important skill is to have a flexible mind that can cope with all these changes while keeping its balance.
